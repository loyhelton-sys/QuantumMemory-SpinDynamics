# 7.5–15–7.5 us comparison with the selected 5–10–5 us control

Reference: `Results/5-10-5/original_three_pulse_rase_optimization_5us_10us_5us/adaptive_targets/spike_cleanup/gaussian4/cleaned_result.jld2`.

New output directory:
`Results/7.5-15-7.5/original_three_pulse_rase_optimization_7p5us_15us_7p5us/comparison_5_10_5/`.

The historical 30 us optimized control provides the starting amplitudes and
phase. Both configurations use the same 0.025 us control interval, 101×101
ensemble, physical distributions, target detuning window, initial spin, and
5 us pre/post free evolution. Preparation asserts these equalities.

Gaussian phase smoothing is adjusted by bisection to match the selected
reference's raw `Jsmooth=-0.0628345485441893`. Its width is approximately
4.325057808 samples (0.108126445 us). The mask is fixed from the starting
control using the same gate definition as before. Phase is then held fixed
while nonnegative amplitudes are optimized. This prevents frequency spikes
from being regenerated by phase updates.

Population acceptance uses `Jpop_inside > -0.0099999` and
`Jpop_outside > -0.0099999`, allowing margin relative to the user's -0.01
threshold. `Jsmooth` must remain within 1e-7 of its matched value. Among
accepted candidates, the highest coherence is saved. Better population values
are not intentionally degraded; the final comparison reports actual values.

This is a matched *summed* smoothness comparison, not a per-duration average.
The 30 us waveform has 1200 samples, versus 800 for 20 us. No global optimum
is claimed; both runs use fixed-phase amplitude refinement after smoothing.

The transient user service `spindynamics-comparable-30us.service` runs the
optimization and final plotting. `status.txt` and `run.log` track progress.
The cap is 50000 new outer iterations, excluding historical iterations and
backtracking/validation evaluations. It can stop earlier after eight failed
searches or coherence gain below 1e-7 over 1000 feasible iterations.

Scripts:

- `examples/prepare_comparable_30us_rase.jl`: prepare the matched smooth seed.
- `examples/run_comparable_30us_rase.jl`: run/resume optimization, then finalize.
- `examples/optimize_comparable_30us_rase.jl`: bounded optimizer.
- `examples/finish_comparable_30us_rase.jl`: 32-substep comparison and figures.

Do not run another optimizer concurrently on this output directory.

At successful completion, `final_result.jld2` stores the selected control and
verified metrics; `comparison.csv` compares both durations at 32 substeps.
`final_figures/` contains pulse, Sz, and coherence timeline images and their
underlying plot data. The 30 us pulse runs from 5 to 35 us in the timeline,
with the echo at 40 us and the displayed timeline ending at 45 us.
The plotted coherence C is the square root of Jcoh at the echo.
